Delete Your Account

Recall - Study Tracker App

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account and all associated data directly from the app:

  1. Open Recall and sign in to your account
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon in the bottom right)
  3. Tap Account
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted

What Data is Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently deleted:

  1. Account data — your name, email address, and authentication credentials
  2. Study data — all subjects, chapters, flashcards, past papers, study sessions, and revision schedules
  3. Timer data — all timer sessions and study time records
  4. AI-generated content — all AI flashcards and AI insights
  5. Preferences — theme, timezone, weekly goal, and app settings
  6. Purchase history — credit balance and subscription status (note: active subscriptions must be cancelled separately through Google Play)

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. We recommend exporting your data from Settings before deleting your account.

Cancel Your Subscription

If you have an active Recall+ subscription, deleting your account does not automatically cancel it. To avoid being charged, cancel your subscription first:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app
  2. Tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions
  3. Find Recall+ and tap Cancel subscription
