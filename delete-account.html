Delete Account & Data

Recall - Study Tracker App

Option 1: Delete from the App

  1. Open Recall and sign in
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon)
  3. Tap Account
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm when prompted

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app, you can request account and data deletion by emailing us. Include the email address associated with your Recall account.

Request Account Deletion

We will process your request and delete all data within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once complete.

What Data is Deleted

All of the following is permanently deleted:

  1. Account data (name, email, credentials)
  2. Study data (subjects, chapters, flashcards, past papers, sessions, revisions)
  3. Timer data (all study time records)
  4. AI-generated content (flashcards and insights)
  5. Preferences (theme, timezone, weekly goal)
  6. Credit balance and purchase history

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. We recommend exporting your data from Settings before requesting deletion.

Cancel Your Subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel active subscriptions. Cancel first via Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Recall+.
